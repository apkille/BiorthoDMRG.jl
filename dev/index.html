BiorthoDMRG.BiorthoDMRG — Module
BiorthoDMRG

Two-site biorthogonal DMRG for non-Hermitian MPOs. With |ψ⟩ = P{ℓψ}|φψ⟩ and |χ⟩ = P{ℓχ}|φχ⟩ in the biorthonormal gauge P{ℓχ}†P{ℓψ} = 1, each bond update solves

P_{ℓχ}† H P_{ℓψ} |φ_ψ⟩ = E |φ_ψ⟩,     P_{ℓψ}† H† P_{ℓχ} |φ_χ⟩ = Ē |φ_χ⟩,

normalizes ⟨φχ|φψ⟩ = 1, and refactorizes both blocks by an oblique projection.

BiorthoDMRG.AbstractBiorthoProjMPO — Type
AbstractBiorthoProjMPO

Supertype for operators projected onto the open window of a biorthogonal pair (chi, psi). Subtypes implement ITensorMPS.position!(P, chi, psi, ℓ) to move the window to sites ℓ:ℓ+1, ITensors.product(P, v) to apply the projected operator to v, and adjoint_product(P, v) to apply its adjoint. P(v) is the same as product(P, v).

BiorthoDMRG.BiorthoObserver — Type
BiorthoObserver(; H=nothing, residual_tol=0.0)

Observer for biorthodmrg that records one entry per sweep. energies holds the energy, conditions the condition number ‖chi‖‖psi‖/|⟨chi|psi⟩|, and stats the SweepStats.

If H is given, residuals holds the relative residuals of psi and chi, computed with H and its adjoint Hdag, and the run stops after the first sweep in which both are below residual_tol. Without H, the run never stops early.

Residuals are used instead of energy changes because the energy need not converge monotonically. They resolve values only down to about √eps, so residual_tol must be larger than that.

BiorthoDMRG.BiorthoProjMPO — Type
BiorthoProjMPO(H::MPO)

H projected onto the open window, with chi as the bra and psi as the ket. H is the full MPO; env caches the left and right environments of ⟨chi|H|psi⟩ outside the window. BiorthoProjMPO(H) creates an empty cache, and the first position! fills it.

BiorthoDMRG.BiorthoProjMPS — Type
BiorthoProjMPS(Lref::MPS, Rref::MPS)

The rank-one operator |Rref⟩⟨Lref| projected onto the open window, with chi as the bra and psi as the ket. It is the deflation term for a converged pair (Lref, Rref); BiorthoProjMPO_MPS applies the weight.

Lref and Rref are the reference states. cacheL caches the environments of ⟨Lref|psi⟩ and cacheR those of ⟨Rref|chi⟩. braL is ⟨Lref| restricted to the window through psi's environments, and braR is ⟨Rref| restricted through chi's; position! recomputes both.

BiorthoProjMPS(Lref, Rref) rescales Rref so that ⟨Lref|Rref⟩ = 1, gives both references new link indices so they cannot collide with those of chi or psi, and creates empty caches.

BiorthoDMRG.EnvCache — Type
EnvCache(N)

Left and right environments of an N-site chain around an open window.

LR[k] holds the left environment of sites 1:k for k ≤ lpos and the right environment of sites k:N for k ≥ rpos; sites lpos+1:rpos-1 form the window. Each environment contracts the ket, the operator, and the conjugated bra over its sites. The operator is an MPO, or the identity when it is nothing.

BiorthoDMRG.MPIBiorthoSum — Type
MPIBiorthoSum(term, comm; mode, checksync = false)

Projected operator whose terms are spread over the ranks of comm. Each rank holds one part of the Hamiltonian, for example term = BiorthoProjMPO(H_p) with H = Σ_p H_p, and builds the environments for that part only. Every rank must call the constructor.

With mode = :root, only rank 0 runs biorthodmrg, and the other ranks run serve!. Rank 0 sends the other ranks every updated site tensor and every window vector, and collects the sum of their products. Every decision of the eigensolver and the truncation is therefore made on rank 0 alone, and the mode does not depend on reproducible arithmetic. Rank 0 calls stopservice! when it is done, for example in a finally block, which releases the other ranks.

With mode = :replicated, every rank runs biorthodmrg on the same inputs. The sum of the products is returned to every rank, and each rank repeats the eigensolver and the truncation on identical data. The ranks stay in step only if their arithmetic is bitwise reproducible, which in practice means the same CPU type and a fixed number of BLAS threads. With checksync = true, each position! compares the newly replaced site tensors across ranks and throws if they differ. Wrap the observer in MPIObserver and set outputlevel = 0 on every rank except 0.

The terms may have Float64 entries on some ranks and ComplexF64 entries on others. Each product is promoted to the common entry type before the sum is taken.

BiorthoDMRG.SweepStats — Type
SweepStats

Worst-case diagnostics over the bonds of one sweep.

maxtruncerr is the largest truncation error of either state, sigma_min the smallest cosine between the kept left and right bases, cosangle_min the smallest cosine between the local left and right eigenvectors before normalization, and maxresidual the largest relative residual of a local left or right eigenvector. BiorthoDMRG.BiorthoProjMPO_MPS — Method
BiorthoProjMPO_MPS(H::MPO, refs::Vector{Tuple{MPS,MPS}}; weight)

H plus deflation terms for converged eigenpairs: the BiorthoProjSum of BiorthoProjMPO(H) with coefficient 1 and one BiorthoProjMPS(L_i, R_i) per pair with coefficient weight. Each pair is (L_i, R_i) = (chi_i, psi_i), in the order biorthodmrg returns them. For exact pairs with nondegenerate E_i, each E_i moves to E_i + weight and every other eigenpair is unchanged, so choose weight to move the converged eigenvalues out of the region selected by which.

BiorthoDMRG._biortho_basis — Method
_biortho_basis(phi_psi, phi_chi, is; biortho_cutoff, tags, svd_kwargs...)

Biorthonormal bases for phi_psi and phi_chi on the indices is.

Each block is truncated by an SVD with svd_kwargs. The two kept bases are then rotated and rescaled, using the SVD of their overlap, so that Xchi† Xpsi is the identity on the new link index, which carries tags.

The singular values of the overlap are the cosines of the angles between the kept subspaces; those at or below biortho_cutoff are dropped. Also returns both truncation spectra and sigma_min, the smallest kept cosine. Dense indices only.

BiorthoDMRG.biorthodmrg — Method
biorthodmrg(H::MPO, psi0::MPS; nsweeps, maxdim, mindim, cutoff, kwargs...)
biorthodmrg(H::MPO, chi0::MPS, psi0::MPS; ...)
biorthodmrg(H::MPO, refs::Vector{Tuple{MPS,MPS}}, chi0::MPS, psi0::MPS; weight, ...)
biorthodmrg(PH::AbstractBiorthoProjMPO, chi0::MPS, psi0::MPS, sweeps::Sweeps; ...)

Two-site DMRG for the left and right eigenvectors of a non-Hermitian H. Returns (E, chi, psi): psi is the right eigenvector and chi the left eigenvector, normalized so that ⟨chi|psi⟩ = 1, and E = ⟨chi|H|psi⟩, all up to truncation.

With one initial state, chi0 = psi0. With refs, previously converged pairs (chi_i, psi_i) are deflated by shifting their eigenvalues by weight. The last form takes any projected operator and a Sweeps object; the others build both.

which selects the eigenvalue as in KrylovKit.eigsolve. target and howmany choose among Ritz pairs as in biorthoproblemsolver. basis chooses :shared (stable, larger bond dimension) or :biorthonormal (compact, unbounded local spectrum); see biorthoreplacebond!. biortho_cutoff drops cosines between the kept left and right bases at or below it, and applies only to :biorthonormal. observer is an ITensorMPS observer or a BiorthoObserver. outputlevel and the eigsolve_* keywords are as in ITensorMPS.dmrg. noise must be zero and indices must be dense.

solver is the local solver: the default solves the two-sided eigenproblem, and sssolver finds a Lindbladian steady state.

BiorthoDMRG.biorthogonalize! — Method
biorthogonalize!(chi, psi, j; normalize=true, kwargs...)

Bring chi and psi into biorthonormal form centered on site j, in place, and return them.

Each bond is refactorized with biorthoreplacebond!, sweeping in from both ends toward j. Afterwards both states share every link index, sites left of j are left-biorthonormal, and sites right of j are right-biorthonormal. Left-biorthonormal means that contracting dag(chi[k]) with psi[k] over their left link and site index gives the identity; right-biorthonormal is the mirror of this.

With normalize=true, chi[j] is rescaled so that ⟨chi|psi⟩ = 1. kwargs are passed to biorthoreplacebond!. Without truncation, the states are unchanged when, at every bond, the kept bases of chi and psi have the same dimension and no overlap cosine is dropped. Otherwise they are truncated.

BiorthoDMRG.biorthoproblemsolver — Method
biorthoproblemsolver(P, phi_psi, phi_chi; which=:SR, target=:which, howmany, tol, krylovdim, maxiter, verbosity)

Solve the local eigenproblem of the projected operator P from both sides. Arnoldi on P, starting from phi_psi, gives E and the right eigenvector; Arnoldi on the adjoint, starting from phi_chi and targeted at conj(E), gives the left eigenvector.

target=:which takes the first right pair in KrylovKit's which order. target=:overlap takes the right pair, among howmany, that overlaps most with the current left block. tol, krylovdim, maxiter and verbosity are passed to KrylovKit.eigsolve.

Returns (E, phi_psi, phi_chi, info), normalized so that ⟨phi_chi|phi_psi⟩ = 1 with equal norms. info.cosangle is the cosine of the angle between the two vectors before normalization, which vanishes at an exceptional point of P. info.residual is the larger of the relative residuals of the two vectors.

BiorthoDMRG.biorthoreplacebond! — Method
biorthoreplacebond!(chi, psi, ℓ, phi_chi, phi_psi; ortho, basis=:biorthonormal, biortho_cutoff=0, svd_kwargs...)

Split the two-site blocks phi_chi and phi_psi back into sites ℓ and ℓ+1 of chi and psi, truncating with svd_kwargs. With ortho="left", site ℓ receives the new bases and site ℓ+1 the remainders, so the window moves right; ortho="right" does the reverse. Both states get the same new link index.

basis=:shared gives both states one orthonormal basis that captures both blocks. The local operator is then an orthogonal compression of H, whose eigenvalues stay within the numerical range of H. basis=:biorthonormal gives each state its own basis, which is more compact, but the local operator then has eigenvalues that can lie far outside the spectrum of H.

Returns the spectra of both blocks and sigma_min, which is 1 for :shared. For :biorthonormal, if no cosine is dropped and both blocks keep the same dimension, the relative error of each block is at most (1 + 1/sigma_min) * sqrt(truncerr).

residuals(H, chi, psi, E; Hdag=adjoint_mpo(H)) -> (r_psi, r_chi)

BiorthoDMRG.sssolver — Method
sssolver(P, phi_psi, phi_chi; tol, krylovdim, maxiter, verbosity)

Local solver for the steady state of a Lindbladian, used as biorthodmrg(𝓛, id; solver = sssolver) with id = vectorized_identity(d).

phi_chi is the local block of the vectorized identity, which is an exact left null vector of P, so the steady state is a right null vector and there is no eigenvalue to select. The solver writes it as x = x₀ + y, with x₀ = phi_psi rescaled to unit trace and y orthogonal to phi_chi, and solves P y = -P x₀ with GMRES inside that orthogonal complement, so the trace stays fixed. phi_chi is returned unchanged and x is normalized so that ⟨phi_chi|x⟩ = 1. The returned value is the Rayleigh quotient of x, zero at convergence, and info.residual is ‖P x‖ / ‖x‖.

ising_imaginary_field(sites; J = 1.0, hx, hz)

Transverse-field Ising chain with an imaginary longitudinal field. Each bond carries -J Z_j Z_{j+1} and each site -hx X_j - i hz Z_j, with Pauli X, Z. BiorthoDMRG.Models.lindbladian — Method
lindbladian(H::MPO, jumps::Vector{ITensor}, d; cutoff = 1e-14)

The Lindbladian ρ ↦ -i[H, ρ] + Σₖ (Lₖ ρ Lₖ† - ½{Lₖ†Lₖ, ρ}) as an MPO acting on vectorized density matrices on the doubled sites d = doubled_siteinds(s). H is an MPO on the physical sites s, and each jump operator is an ITensor on a single physical site, for example sqrt(γ) * op("S-", s[1]). The terms are summed and compressed with cutoff.

The steady state is the right null vector with unit trace. We can find it with biorthodmrg(𝓛, vectorized_identity(d); solver = sssolver).

The left eigenvector is vectorized_identity(d), and tr(Oρ) is inner(vectorized_identity(d), apply(O, rho)).

A single-site "C" carries no Jordan–Wigner string, so fermionic jump operators should be written in a spin representation.

BiorthoDMRG.Models.nonreciprocal_ssh — Method
nonreciprocal_ssh(sites; t1, t2, γ, V = 0.0)

SSH chain of spinless fermions with non-reciprocal intracell hopping, sites ordered A₁, B₁, A₂, B₂, …. Cell j carries (t1 + γ/2) c†_{A_j} c_{B_j} + (t1 - γ/2) c†_{B_j} c_{A_j}, neighboring cells are joined by t2 (c†_{B_j} c_{A_{j+1}} + h.c.), and V adds V n_x n_{x+1} on every bond. For |t1| > |γ|/2 it is similar to the Hermitian chain with intracell hopping sign(t1) * sqrt(t1^2 - γ^2/4), so the spectra agree. Use "Fermion" sites; the number of sites must be even.

BiorthoDMRG.Models.xxz — Method
xxz(sites; Δ, g = 0.0, J = 1.0, h = 0.0)

XXZ chain with non-reciprocal spin flips. Each bond carries J/2 (e^g S⁺_{j+1} S⁻_j + e^-g S⁻_{j+1} S⁺_j) + Δ Sᶻ_j Sᶻ_{j+1} and each site -h Sᶻ_j. For any g it equals S H₀ S⁻¹ with H₀ the Hermitian chain at g = 0 and S = exp(g Σ_j j Sᶻ_j), so the spectra agree, and the right and left eigenvectors are S|ψ₀⟩ and S⁻¹|ψ₀⟩. Under Jordan–Wigner it is the interacting Hatano–Nelson model. Use "S=1/2" sites.
